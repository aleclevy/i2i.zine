# Our Mission

i2i zine's mission is to amplify the narratives of queer artists in the greater LA community through grass-roots publishing, archival work, and event planning. Since 2024 we have been curating zines on topics ranging from the local art scene to queer history and social activism, and have been bringing people together through community events and fundraisers. Everyone's voice deserves to be heard, and through open calls for art, prose, and performance, we aim to make these stories accessible to all. Art is for everyone!

### UNSILENCED: The TTI Issue

In 2025, i2i had the chance to collaborate with the nonprofit **UNSILENCED**. For those wondering: "Unsilenced aims to end institutional child abuse within the Troubled Teen Industry (TTI), hold abusive programs accountable, and support survivors in their pursuit of justice and healing." You can check out the incredible work they are doing at <a href="https://www.unsilenced.org/" target="_blank">unsilenced.org</a>.

Through a combination of documentation of the history of the Troubled Teen Industry, and testimony and art from 6 victims of the TTI itself, i2i created **i2i 4.5: UNSILENCED**. The zine was released at an event at Recreation Coffee in Long Beach, and succeeded in spreading awareness and garnering donations.
