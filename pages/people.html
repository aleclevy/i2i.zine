Ellie Kerns

Founder & Director

Ellie Kerns is an organizer from Long Beach, CA who believes community action works best when it's backed by artists. Driven by a desire to document queer spaces in the 21st century, Kerns founded i2i in an attempt to immortalize the community of artists blossoming around them.

@ellie.kerns

Noelle Averett

Co-Director

Noelle Averett is a Long Beach-based visual artist working in drawing, sculpture, and quilting. She is deeply passionate about collaboration, and utilizes her past experience in editorial illustration and public programming to uplift her community's voices. Averett is also a founding member of Little A Collective.

@ohnoelle · noelleaverett.com

Lena Zelenz

Art Director

Lena Zelenz is a Long Beach-based artist who recently graduated from CSULB with a degree in Studio Art. Drawing is her primary focus, but she also works with paint, fiber, ceramics and more. She is inspired by the natural world with an interest in biology and most loves to draw animals and plants.

@mothgirlll

Sajah Francesca

Editorial Director

Sajah Francesca is a poet from Long Beach, CA whose writing emphasizes queerness, surrealism, and nihilistic optimism. They explore their artistic expression through the avenues of poetry, prose, and scriptwriting by manipulating structure and blending high and low forms of language. They induce their writing with an equal balance of darkness and whimsy, mirroring conflicting realities of the world, creating space for reflection, and promoting the exploration of serenity through the analysis of the soul. Their writing indulges in satirical criticism as a way to comment on society, government, social media, and other facets of life. Sajah Francesca's debut poetry collection, titled "Nasty. Unpredictable. Nonsense." was published in June 2024 by Archway Publishing. Additional publications include: just femme & dandy- the Survive Ed., Contemporary Jo Issue IV, The Shallot Vol. 2, Issue 1., i2i Zine Ed. 1 and Ed. 2.

@sajahfrancesca

Adam Ruilova

Event Coordinator

Adam Ruilova is an organizer from Long Beach, CA known for his experience in community organizing, event planning, and presence in the LA ballroom scene. He is a member of the West Coast chapter of the House of Icon. He has been an associate of i2i since its inception in 2024.
